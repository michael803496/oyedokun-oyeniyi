Profile

Hon. Oyedokun Oyeniyi Timothy, popularly known as OOT, is a seasoned administrator, strategic manager and respected political leader with a proven record of service, innovation and community development. He hails from Ogbomoso South Local Government, Oyo State, and has distinguished himself across public service, corporate management and grassroots leadership.

A dynamic communicator and strategic thinker, he is widely admired for his integrity, resilience and developmental vision. His leadership blends ethical decision-making, financial prudence and innovation, qualities that have earned him respect within Oyo State political circles and beyond.
